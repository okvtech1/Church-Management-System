This Privacy Policy explains how OKV Technology Consults ("OKV," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when an organization ("Organization," "Church," "you," or "your") and its Admins, Users, and their congregation members use the OKV Church Management System (the "Service").

Please read this policy alongside our Terms of Service. By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. Who we are

OKV Technology Consults is a Nigerian technology consultancy that builds and operates the Service. We are the party responsible for the Service's infrastructure and, in most cases, act as a data processor on behalf of the Organizations that use it (see Section 10 for what that means in practice).

Contact: technologyokv@gmail.com · +2348104141138 · www.okvtechnology.com

2. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to three groups of people, and treats them a little differently:

  • Organization Admins and Users — the people who sign up for and log into the Service on behalf of a church or ministry.
  • Congregation members and other individuals whose details (names, contact information, attendance, giving records, etc.) an Organization enters into the Service. These individuals do not have accounts and never interact with OKV directly — their data is entered, viewed, and managed entirely by the Organization.
  • Visitors to our marketing site, pricing page, and live demo, who are not yet registered.

3. Data we collect

a) Account & billing data

  • Organization name, Admin full name, email address, phone number, and a hashed password (we never store your password in plain text).
  • Subscription plan, billing cycle, and subscription/trial dates.
  • For manual bank-transfer payments: the full name, email, phone number, plan, and (if you choose to attach one) a screenshot of your payment, which you submit for our team to verify by hand.
  • For gateway payments (where enabled, e.g. Paystack/Flutterwave): a transaction reference that we use to verify payment status with the gateway. We do not collect or store your card number, CVV, or full bank details — those are handled directly by the payment gateway.

b) Data you enter into the Service

As an Organization, the modules you use may hold personal data about your members and staff, which can include: names, contact details, addresses, family/household relationships, attendance records, tithes, offerings and pledge/giving history, ministry and department assignments, event and volunteer records, and any notes your team chooses to record. You control what is entered here — OKV does not decide what data an Organization collects about its own members.

c) Usage & technical data

  • Login times, session activity, and basic device/browser information, used for security (e.g. detecting suspicious logins) and troubleshooting.
  • Standard web server logs generated by the platforms we run on (Google Apps Script, Google Drive/ Sheets, and wherever the front-end pages are hosted).

4. How and where data is stored

The Service is built on Google Sheets and Google Apps Script. Each Organization gets its own dedicated, private Google Spreadsheet for its member and operational data — Organizations do not share a spreadsheet with one another. A separate, small "Master" spreadsheet holds only account-level information (organization registry, plan/subscription status, and platform settings) and never holds member, attendance, or financial records.

Because the Service runs on Google's infrastructure, your data benefits from Google's own security and data-center protections. OKV does not operate separate physical servers for this Service; we build and configure the application layer that runs on top of Google's platform.

5. How we use data

  • To create and operate your account, and to keep each Organization's data isolated from every other Organization.
  • To process and verify subscription payments, and to send you renewal, trial, and payment-status emails.
  • To send messages that your own Organization chooses to send to its members (announcements, birthday/anniversary messages, etc.) through the email, SMS, or WhatsApp channels you configure or that your Admin enables.
  • To respond to support requests and communicate important service updates.
  • To monitor and maintain the security, performance, and storage capacity of the Service (for example, tracking how close an Organization's spreadsheet is to Google Sheets' cell limit, so we can warn our team before it becomes a problem — this is visible only to OKV's Super Admin, never to Organizations).
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use member data entered by Organizations for our own advertising or marketing purposes.

6. Our legal basis for processing

We process personal data under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) 2019, on the following bases: performance of the contract we have with you (providing the Service you signed up for), your consent (for example, when you submit payment details or opt into messaging), and our legitimate interest in keeping the Service secure, reliable, and within Google's technical limits.

7. Sharing data with third parties

We share data only where necessary to run the Service:

  • Google — as the infrastructure provider for Sheets, Apps Script, Drive, and Gmail (for transactional email).
  • Payment gateways (e.g. Paystack, Flutterwave, Remita) — only if your Organization or OKV enables gateway payments, and only to verify a transaction you initiated.
  • SMS and WhatsApp providers — only if configured, and only to deliver messages your Organization sends through those channels.
  • Law enforcement or regulators — where required by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of OKV, our customers, or the public.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data to advertisers or data brokers.

8. Data retention

We retain account and member data for as long as an Organization's subscription is active. If an account is cancelled or lapses, we retain the data for a reasonable period afterward in case the Organization wishes to reactivate, after which it may be permanently deleted. An Organization Admin may request export or deletion of their Organization's data at any time by contacting us (see Section 16).

9. Data security

  • Each Organization's data lives in its own spreadsheet, isolated from every other Organization.
  • Passwords are hashed before they are ever transmitted or stored — we do not have access to your plain-text password.
  • All communication with the Service happens over HTTPS.
  • Access to a given Organization's data is restricted to the Users that Organization's Admin has explicitly created and assigned roles to.

No system can be guaranteed 100% secure. If we become aware of a data breach affecting your information, we will notify affected Organizations without undue delay, in line with our obligations under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

10. Organizations, members & who's responsible for what

For the personal data of congregation members and other individuals that an Organization enters into the Service, the Organization is the data controller — it decides what data to collect about its members and why. OKV acts as a data processor, providing the platform that stores and processes that data on the Organization's behalf and instructions.

This means: if you are a member of a church using this Service and have questions or requests about your personal data, your first point of contact should be that church's Administrator, not OKV directly — though we're glad to help route the request if needed.

11. Data about children

Some Organizations use the Service to manage children's ministry records. Responsibility for obtaining any necessary parental or guardian consent before entering a child's information rests with the Organization, as the data controller. OKV does not knowingly collect personal data directly from children through this Service — all data is entered by adult Organization staff.

12. Your rights

Depending on your role, you may have the right to: access the personal data held about you; request correction of inaccurate data; request deletion of your data; object to or restrict certain processing; and request a copy of your data in a portable format. Organization Admins and Users can exercise these rights for their own account data directly through the dashboard (e.g. editing your profile, changing your password) or by contacting us. Individual congregation members should route requests through their Organization's Admin, per Section 10.

13. Cookies & local storage

The Service does not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser's local storage and IndexedDB to keep you logged in between visits and to support offline use — letting the app keep working and sync your changes automatically once you're back online. This data stays on your device and is used only to operate the app.

14. International data transfers

Because the Service runs on Google's global infrastructure, data may be processed or stored on servers located outside Nigeria, subject to Google's own data protection commitments. Where personal data is transferred internationally, we rely on the safeguards Google provides as an infrastructure processor.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the Service or applicable law. We'll update the "Last Updated" date above when we do, and for significant changes we'll make a reasonable effort to notify Organization Admins by email.
