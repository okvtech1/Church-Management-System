About The System

One system for your whole church, from the front desk to the finance office

OKV Church Management System replaces scattered spreadsheets, paper registers and WhatsApp broadcasts with one organized place for membership records, attendance, tithes & offerings, ministries, pastoral care, events and communications — built specifically around how churches actually run.

It's built offline-first: your data lives on your device first, so a weak network on a Sunday morning never stops you from taking attendance or recording a member's details. When you're back online, everything syncs automatically.

How It Works

Offline when you need it. Online when you want it.

Offline Mode
1
Install the app once — it works like any app on your device, no browser or internet required afterward.
2
Add, edit and manage every record right on the device — all data is saved instantly, even in airplane mode.
3
Nothing is lost — every record stays safely on your device until you're ready to sync.
Online Mode
1
Sign in from any device on your team, with role-based access for Admins and Users.
2
Changes sync automatically in the background — or tap Sync any time — with a notification when it's done.
3
Everyone on your team sees the same up-to-date information, scoped safely to your organization only.
