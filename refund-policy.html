Refund Policy

Effective Date & Last Updated: August 19, 2026

This Refund Policy is part of our Terms of Service and explains how refunds work for OKV Church Management System subscriptions.

Free trial

Every new Organization gets a 7-day free trial with no payment required to start. If you decide the Service isn't for you, simply let the trial lapse — you won't be charged anything.

How payments work

Subscriptions are activated either by a manual bank transfer that our team verifies by hand, or through an online payment gateway where enabled. Either way, you choose when to pay — there's no automatic card charge you'd need a refund to reverse.

When a refund is available

Generally refundable:
  • A duplicate or accidental payment (e.g. you paid twice for the same billing cycle).
  • A payment made in error (e.g. wrong plan or wrong organization selected).
  • Service was unavailable to you for a significant period due to a fault on our side, and we were unable to resolve it within a reasonable time.
Generally not refundable:
  • Change of mind after a paid billing cycle has started and the Service has been actively used.
  • Partial-month or partial-cycle cancellations — we don't prorate refunds for time you choose not to use within a cycle you already paid for.
  • Add-ons or configuration work already delivered (e.g. custom setup you asked us to do).

We review refund requests individually and may make exceptions at our discretion, including partial refunds, where the circumstances warrant it.

How to request a refund

Contact us with your Organization name, the payment date, and the reason for your request:

  • technologyokv@gmail.com
  • +2348104141138

We aim to respond to every refund request within 2 business days.

Processing time

Approved refunds for bank transfer payments are returned to the same account the payment came from, typically within 5–10 business days. Refunds for gateway payments (Paystack/Flutterwave, where used) are processed back through that gateway and follow its own settlement timelines, which are outside our control.

Chargebacks

If you have a billing concern, please contact us first — most issues can be resolved faster directly than through a card chargeback or bank dispute, and it helps us keep your account in good standing while we sort it out.
